Enteral Feeding Device · Digital Twin

Watches the assembly lines, warns before the first out-of-spec unit is made, and keeps every record tamper-proof.

What the system does, in six steps

1

Receive data

Every second the simulator sends bag port sizes (BAG-01), tube sizes (PVC-01) and one record per finished device from each assembly line (ASM-01/02/03).

2

Store it tamper-proof

Each record is saved exactly as received in a ledger. Records can be added but never changed or deleted, and a hash chain proves it.

3

Work out the expected depth

A simple formula says how deep the tube should go for this bag lot, tube reel and pressure: the expected depth.

4

Watch the difference

Measured minus expected depth is just noise on a healthy line. The drift alarm meter spots when it keeps moving one way: drift.

5

Forecast and lock

For every unit the system computes the chance that the next one falls outside 13–17 mm (expected depth plus the smoothed recent drift). When it reaches 10 %, the unit 3 ahead is locked with a timestamp before it is made. The jury scores the error E = |Z − X| and the lead time L = X − Y.

6

Check against reality

When the camera measures that unit, the system records whether the prediction was a HIT, EARLY, LATE or MISSED.

The one thing we measure: insertion depth

How far the PVC tube is pushed into the bag port. The camera measures it on every finished device.

too shallow
< 13.0 mm
OK · 13.0 – 17.0 mm
target 15.0 mm
too deep
> 17.0 mm

1.5 cm ± 0.2 cm. A tube that is too shallow can leak; one that is too deep can block the port. Depth depends on the bag port size (bigger → deeper), the tube size (bigger → shallower), the pressure (higher → deeper) and on how well the machine is calibrated.

Real data vs synthetic data

REAL Historical and Realtime data come from the simulator API and are stored untouched. The historical line never failed (closest unit: 0.149 mm from the limit).

SYNTHETIC To show and test predictions we also use synthetic data: real historical units with an artificial drift added. It is always labelled and kept in a separate file.

FAKE The Model lab can generate fake data on the spot with a drift you choose. It exists only in memory.

How the models were tested

Same 1,800 real units, same inputs (bag port size, tube size, pressure, all known before the device is made), four tests:

Random split

Hide a random 20 % of units, predict them. The easiest test: similar units are in training.

New lots

Hide one whole bag lot and tube reel. Does the model work on the next production lot?

Unseen setting

Hide a whole pressure, bag size or tube size. Can it handle a setting it has never seen? more
